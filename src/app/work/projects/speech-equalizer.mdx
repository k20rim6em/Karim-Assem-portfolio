## Overview

Podcast and lecture recordings are often noisy and sound muddy or muffled. This project (CIE 247, Digital Signal Processing) builds a full MATLAB pipeline that cleans up a noisy voice recording and reshapes its frequency content for clarity, output as a ready-to-listen `.wav` file.

## What I built

- A spectral-subtraction noise reduction stage that estimates the room's noise profile from a silent stretch of audio and subtracts it from the signal
- A 7-band parallel filter bank (both FIR window-based and IIR Butterworth/Chebyshev options) tuned to speech: rumble, warmth, vowel resonance, clarity, presence, sibilance, and air
- Per-band gain control, recombined and normalized into a single equalized output
- Zero-phase filtering throughout to avoid smearing consonants and plosives

## Result

Applying a "speech clarity" gain profile (cutting rumble/muddiness, boosting the 800 Hz–10 kHz presence range) suppressed the background noise floor by 20–30 dB while keeping vocal formants intact, producing a noticeably clearer, more intelligible recording.

**Tools:** MATLAB, spectral subtraction, FIR/IIR filter banks
